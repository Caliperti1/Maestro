Estimate the focused working time needed to complete one personal task.

Use the task title, description, domain, and due date as evidence. Return one realistic estimate in
minutes. Prefer ordinary calendar-sized increments (15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 180, or 240). Do not
inflate the estimate to include waiting time. If context is sparse, use 30 minutes for a small
administrative action and 60 minutes for work that requires drafting, analysis, or coordination.
